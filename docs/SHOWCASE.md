# Range–Voxel 3D Detection: qualitative examples

These author-provided Waymo figures illustrate RV-SDTM detections in two
challenging settings: sparse returns at long range and partial occlusion.
They accompany the [measured validation results](RESULTS.md).

In both figures, the **upper row** is the FSHNet comparison and the **lower
row** is RV-SDTM. Green boxes denote predictions, red boxes denote ground
truth, and blue dashed outlines highlight selected objects.

## Figure provenance

The images were supplied with the authors' manuscript materials. They are
selected qualitative comparisons and are not produced automatically by the
training or evaluation commands in this release. Aggregate performance and
evaluation protocols are documented in [Results](RESULTS.md).

# Qualitative Waymo examples

These two author-provided figures show selected scenes, not a quantitative
evaluation. They are not generated by the training or evaluation commands in
this source release. The [measured results](RESULTS.md) are authoritative for
accuracy claims.

Within each figure, the upper row shows the manuscript's FSHNet comparison and
the lower row shows RV-SDTM. Green boxes are predictions, red boxes are ground
truth, and blue dashed outlines mark the discussed objects.

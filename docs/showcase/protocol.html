Interpretation & provenance

Models and scope

These author-supplied Waymo visualizations are rendered from recorded model predictions. RV-SDTM uses fshnet_sdtm_best.yaml and checkpoint_epoch_12_SDTM_best.pth. The comparison uses fshnet_light.yaml and checkpoint_epoch_12_FSHNet_light.pth. “FSHNet” in the figure is a display alias for FSHNet-Light. The source records report complete loading of 902/902 and 377/377 tensors, respectively.

The visualization checkpoint hash begins 248718635f90. The separately archived Waymo evaluation uses checkpoint f67a27ef0def, documented in docs/ARCHIVED_RESULTS.md. Full original identities and source-frame mappings are preserved in metadata.json.

GT-coverage diagnostic

Green rectangles are GT matched only by RV-SDTM, red only by FSHNet-Light, light gray by both, and dark gray by neither. All valid GT in the display area remain visible. Rectangles use GT geometry. The gold outline locates the right-hand detail panel. The overlay shows GT boxes; complete diagnostic counts, including unmatched predictions, are retained in the CSV.

Both models use display score threshold 0.5, their own training data configurations, and native postprocessing, aligned by frame. Same-class one-to-one 3D IoU matching uses Vehicle / Pedestrian / Cyclist thresholds 0.7 / 0.5 / 0.5. GT must have at least one LiDAR point. The display area is the XY square |x|, |y| ≤ 75 m. Coverage status reflects both same-class detection and the specified IoU matching criterion.

The displayed vehicle-scene frame 0146 maximizes RV-SDTM-only minus baseline-only GT matches over 30 candidate frames, with earlier frames breaking ties. Counts are 36 RV-SDTM-only, 8 baseline-only, 70 both, and 32 neither: a selected-frame net difference of +28. The complete diagnostic CSV retains all 30 frames, including unmatched prediction counts.

Prediction videos and timing

Blue boxes are RV-SDTM predictions at score threshold 0.5, retained from the model's native postprocessing. Class-focused clips highlight Cyclist, Pedestrian, and Vehicle detections across distant-road-user and dense-traffic scenes.

Each of the four scenes contains 30 consecutive source frames at 10 Hz. The 12-second BEV video joins the four distinct scenes with a fixed bird's-eye camera; each source frame is repeated three times in a 30 fps video export. Cuts separate the four source scenes.

Each 13-second multi-view video presents the same source clip as an elevated oblique view, low-angle replay, final-frame camera orbit, and BEV replay. FROZEN-FRAME ORBIT marks a stationary detection frame with camera movement. The multi-view and BEV videos share the same four scenes.

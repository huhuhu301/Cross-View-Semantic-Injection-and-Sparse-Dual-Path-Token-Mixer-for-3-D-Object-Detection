Interpretation & provenance

Models and scope

These are author-supplied, offline Waymo visualizations, not a live detector or new evaluation. RV-SDTM uses fshnet_sdtm_best.yaml and checkpoint_epoch_12_SDTM_best.pth. The comparison uses fshnet_light.yaml and checkpoint_epoch_12_FSHNet_light.pth. “FSHNet” in the figure is a display alias for FSHNet-Light, not FSHNet-Base. The source records report complete loading of 902/902 and 377/377 tensors, respectively.

The visualization checkpoint hash begins 248718635f90; it is not the archived Waymo benchmark checkpoint (f67a27ef0def) in the repository's results table. No official scores for the visualization checkpoint are supplied here. Full original identities and source-frame mappings are preserved in metadata.json.

GT-coverage diagnostic

Green rectangles are GT matched only by RV-SDTM, red only by FSHNet-Light, light gray by both, and dark gray by neither. All valid GT in the display area remain visible. Rectangles use GT geometry, not regressed prediction coordinates. The gold outline locates the right-hand detail panel. Unmatched prediction markers are hidden, not removed from diagnostic counts; the image cannot establish an absence of false positives.

Both models use display score threshold 0.5, their own training data configurations, and native postprocessing, aligned by frame. Same-class one-to-one 3D IoU matching uses Vehicle / Pedestrian / Cyclist thresholds 0.7 / 0.5 / 0.5. GT must have at least one LiDAR point. The display area is the XY square |x|, |y| ≤ 75 m, not a radius-75 m circle. This diagnostic does not implement the complete official AP/APH protocol; an IoU failure can also cause an unmatched GT.

The displayed vehicle-scene frame 0146 maximizes RV-SDTM-only minus baseline-only GT matches over 30 candidate frames, with earlier frames breaking ties. Counts are 36 RV-SDTM-only, 8 baseline-only, 70 both, and 32 neither: a selected-frame net difference of +28, not a validation-set gain. The complete diagnostic CSV retains all 30 frames, including unmatched prediction counts.

Prediction videos and timing

Blue boxes are actual RV-SDTM predictions at score threshold 0.5. The supplied videos do not apply GT-based filtering, extra NMS, tracking, target interpolation, or generated boxes.

Each of the four scenes contains 30 consecutive source frames at 10 Hz. The 12-second BEV video joins the four distinct scenes with a fixed bird's-eye camera; each source frame is repeated three times in a 30 fps render. Scene cuts do not represent a continuous drive.

Each 13-second multi-view video presents the same source clip as an elevated oblique view, low-angle replay, final-frame camera orbit, and BEV replay. FROZEN-FRAME ORBIT marks a stationary detection frame, not a new detection timestep. The multi-view and BEV videos share the same four scenes. Playback FPS is not model inference FPS.

Files and use

The 12 retained media files are byte-identical to the source handoff: five MP4s, six covers/stills, and a BEV GIF. Four redundant multi-view GIFs are omitted; metadata asset references were adjusted accordingly. Original predictions and diagnostic counts are unchanged.
